# Hello there! 👋

I'm **Anugil Velayudhan**, a **Language Analyst (Contract)** at the **International Centre for Free and Open Source Solutions (ICFOSS)**, Government of Kerala, India.

I'm passionate about the intersection of **language, speech, and technology**, with a particular interest in developing computational resources and applying experimental methods to the study of human language.

## 🔬 Research Interests

* Acoustic, Articulatory, and Auditory Phonetics
* Forensic Phonetics and Sociophonetics
* Theoretical and Laboratory Phonology
* Cognitive Phonetics and Phonology
* Language and Accent Bias
* Corpus Linguistics (Speech and Text)
* Morphology, Morphophonology and Morphoprosody
* Speech Technology and Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## 💻 Tools & Technologies

I work primarily with **Praat**, **Python**, and **R** for speech and text data extraction, processing, visualisation, and statistical analysis. My research combines **statistical modelling** and **machine learning** to investigate linguistic patterns and answer research questions.

## 🌱 Research Philosophy

I'm also interested in **open-source language resources**, **reproducible research**, and developing tools that support linguistic research and language technology.
